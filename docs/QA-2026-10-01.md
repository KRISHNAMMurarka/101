## Slashstorm free-stroke correction

The owner then reported that the controller marker felt anchored and that a
new diagonal slash seemed to start from the previous blade position. The game
renderer had drawn its current blade segment even when the cut was not held;
that made the first repositioning frame visible as a false line. Slashstorm now
stores only segments created while dragging, starts each touch at its own
position, and fades recent segments after 0.18 seconds. An idle blade draws
no dot or line. A focused regression covers both directions and a new stroke
after release; it failed against the previous state shape and passes with the
new stroke path.

The native touch marker previously remained visible at the pad center and its
movement was limited to 56 points. It now appears only during contact and
follows the touch across the full pad. The decorative marker cannot become the
touch target, and the PanResponder is kept stable across visual state updates.
The browser swipe surface also hides its idle center marker. Touch down and
release now publish blade position and held state together in one 24-byte
realtime frame rather than two frames each. This reduces packet count at those
edges; no finger-to-pixel timing improvement has been measured.

After a fresh local build and server restart, the host's idle play canvas had
no blade anchor. A physical OnePlus screenshot during a long diagonal gesture
shows the marker partway across the pad:
[touch in motion](receipts/2026-10-01/oneplus-swipe-in-motion.png). The Android
phone remained a connected Sword One, and a run driven by repeated diagonal
swipes reached **298 points** on the rebuilt host. This is automated touch
injection and game response; a person's subjective feel is still open.

## QR scanner and screen glare

The native 101 Link scanner now uses a two-finger pinch to control camera zoom
continuously. Its camera remains in the native automatic focus and exposure
mode. A Light control can illuminate a dark room; it defaults off, which avoids
adding glare when scanning a screen. Scans of unrelated or expired QR codes
leave the scanner open with a recovery message. A valid 101 Link ticket is
integrity-checked before the scanner closes, and repeated scan callbacks cannot
start duplicate connections. Local connection failures now show a retry message
without a raw platform error.

The host QR is displayed at 320 CSS pixels from a 640-pixel image with a
four-module quiet zone. Its black-on-grey modules keep strong contrast while
the background is less bright than white. The same treatment appears in the
game pairing panel and Devices page. The rebuilt Devices page rendered the
larger code: [host QR](receipts/2026-10-01/qr-host-larger.png).

On the physical OnePlus, the scanner opened and fit in portrait
([receipt](receipts/2026-10-01/oneplus-pinch-scanner.png)) and short landscape
([receipt](receipts/2026-10-01/oneplus-scanner-landscape.png)). Its Light control

geometry and connection-error tests failed before implementation and passed
